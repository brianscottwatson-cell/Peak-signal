What it is

A forward deployed engineer is an engineer from Peak Signal who works with your team, inside your tools and your processes. They set the system up with the people who run the work, rather than handing over a report. Peak Signal delivers the engagement itself.

What the AI operating system is

The AI operating system is the client-facing name for the Foundation already described on the homepage: assessment, data readiness, and the AI harness. It is not a new product.

It is one shared layer that connects email, drive, CRM, phone, and other tools so each AI helper works from the full picture. It gives the intelligence layer a holistic view of what you do, who your customers are, and how things work, then plugs your email, drive, CRM, phone system, and any other tools into one unified AI system.

That is an AI operating system for business, and it is AI integration for enterprise teams that want the tools they already have to work as one.

How an engagement runs

Peak Signal does this work itself, in three steps.

Scoping call

A free scoping call to learn the business and see whether the work fits.

Deep-dive assessment

The deep-dive assessment is paid. We learn the systems, the data, and the bottlenecks, then design what to set up.

Deployment

Deployment is the embedded forward deployed engineer engagement. The engineer works with your team and sets up the AI operating system.

Inside deployment, the rhythm is Assess, Recommend, Implement, and Support: technology and AI, chosen and set up for your business.

What your team keeps

Your accounts and your data stay yours. Your email, drive, CRM, and phone accounts stay yours. Owners approve outgoing messages. Drafts are for your approval. It sends on your behalf only if you turn that on. Humans stay in charge.
