AI Deployments is AI implementation for mid-size and enterprise companies in the United States. A forward deployed engineer from Peak Signal works with your team to set up an AI operating system for your business. It starts with a free scoping call: book a 30-minute call.

What it is

A forward deployed engineer is an engineer from Peak Signal who works with your team, inside your tools and your processes. They set the system up with the people who run the work, rather than handing over a report.

What the AI operating system is

The AI operating system is what the homepage already describes: an assessment, data readiness, and the AI Harness. It is not a new product.

It is one shared layer that connects email, drive, CRM, phone, and other tools so each AI helper works from the full picture. It gives the intelligence layer a holistic view of what you do, who your customers are, and how things work, then plugs your email, drive, CRM, phone system, and any other tools into one unified AI system.

That is an AI operating system for business, and it is AI integration for enterprise teams that want the tools they already have to work as one.

How an engagement runs

The engagement runs in three steps.

Scoping call

A free scoping call to learn the business and see whether the work fits.

Setup

The $2,500 one-time setup covers scoping on every plan. A recorded scoping interview becomes your business-context file, along with system connections, your first helpers, and approval rules. There is no separate paid assessment.

Deployment

Deployment is the embedded forward deployed engineer engagement. The engineer works with your team and sets up the AI operating system.

Inside deployment, the rhythm is Assess, Recommend, Implement, and Support: technology and AI, chosen and set up for your business.
