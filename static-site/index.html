Foundation

The groundwork for your AI-optimized business: an assessment, data that's ready to use, and an AI harness that ties every solution together.

Assessment

In an assessment, we meet with you to understand your business, your goals, your systems, the technology you use today, and the bottlenecks you're facing. Then we design a solution that gets you to the outcomes you want.

Data Readiness

We clean and organize your data and processes so the AI can use them.

AI Harness

It gives the intelligence layer a holistic view of what you do, who your customers are, and how things work, then plugs your email, drive, CRM, phone system, and any other tools into one unified AI system.

Solutions

We work with businesses to identify the areas they want to enable with AI. Every client's mix is different, so we put together the website, voice agent, email, quoting, and integrations your business needs, all tied together.

Website

Instant updates with zero friction, plus ongoing SEO and optimization to grow traffic.

Outcome: Your site stays current without waiting on anyone, and more people find you on Google.

Voice

An AI voice agent that answers when you can't, books meetings, handles FAQs, and filters low-priority calls.

Outcome: No lost revenue from missed calls, your time back, and peace of mind.

Email

An agent that watches your inbox, prioritizes what matters, catches important emails you miss, drafts replies for your approval, and automatically sets follow-up reminders.

Outcome: Nothing slips through, the blank-page problem is gone, and your inbox stays organized.

Quoting

A bid generator that turns job details and vendor pricing into a clean, branded, high-fidelity quote in minutes instead of hours. You review it before it goes out.

Outcome: Deals don't die in the queue, and you look like a bigger company.

Integrations

Everything feeds itself through one intelligence layer.

Outcome: Your tools share what they know, so nothing gets entered twice and every agent works from the full picture.

Questions

Straight answers before you book.

What is an assessment, and what does it cost?

An assessment is a meeting to understand your business, goals, systems, current technology, and bottlenecks, so we can design a solution that gets you to the outcomes you want. Book a call to discuss cost.

Does this replace my software?

In most cases, it doesn't replace your software, but helps connect all your existing software.

Does AI send emails without my OK?

No. Drafts are for your approval. It sends on your behalf only if you turn that on.

What happens to a missed call?

An AI voice agent answers when you can't, books meetings, handles FAQs, and filters low-priority calls.

How long does a build take?

It depends on the business. Book an assessment to talk through the work. No timeline is promised here.

Who owns the data and the accounts?

Your email, drive, CRM, and phone accounts stay yours. Peak Signal plugs into them. Information you send through the site is handled as described in the privacy policy.

Where do you work?

Evergreen, Golden, and the Colorado foothills, plus remote.
