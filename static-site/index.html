Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers before you book.

What is an assessment, and what does it cost?

An assessment is a paid engagement where we learn your business, goals, systems, current technology, and bottlenecks, then design a solution that gets you to the outcomes you want. Cost depends on the size of your business. Book a call and we'll scope it with you.

Does this replace my software?

In most cases, it doesn't replace your software, but helps connect all your existing software.

Does AI send emails without my OK?

No. Drafts are for your approval. It sends on your behalf only if you turn that on.

What happens to a missed call?

An AI voice agent answers when you can't, books meetings, handles FAQs, and filters low-priority calls.

How long does a build take?

It depends on the business. Book a call to talk through the work. No timeline is promised here.

Who owns the data and the accounts?

Your email, drive, CRM, and phone accounts stay yours. Peak Signal plugs into them. Information you send through the site is handled as described in the privacy policy.

Where do you work?

Evergreen, Golden, and the Colorado foothills, plus remote.
