What does an AI deployment look like for a small or mid-size business?

Updated Nov 2026

An AI deployment is hands-on setup work: someone learns how your business runs, then connects your email, phone, website, and other tools so AI helpers can answer calls, draft replies, and build quotes from the full picture. At Peak Signal it runs in three steps: a free scoping call, a paid assessment, and then the deployment itself.

What it costs depends on the size of your business and what you want to enable, so cost is scoped after the assessment rather than quoted from a price list.

The three steps

StepWhat happensCost
1. Scoping callA 30-minute conversation to learn the business and see whether the work fitsFree
2. AssessmentWe learn your goals, systems, current technology, data, and bottlenecks, then design what to set upPaid. Depends on the size of your business
3. DeploymentWe set up the system with your team, inside your tools and processes, then support itScoped from the assessment

Inside the deployment, the rhythm is assess, recommend, implement, and support.

What gets set up (every client's mix is different)

  • The foundation: data readiness (cleaning and organizing data and processes so AI can use them) and an AI harness that connects your email, drive, CRM, phone system, and other tools into one system.
  • Website: an AI-first site that's quick to update and feeds the phone agent the same answers.
  • Search and Google: ongoing SEO and a managed Google Business Profile.
  • Phone: an agent that picks up, summarizes each call, and emails the owners.
  • Inbox: an assistant that watches the shop inbox, puts first things first, and drafts replies for your approval.
  • Quotes: a generator that turns job details and your own pricing into a clean, branded quote the owner reviews.
  • Integrations: your tools share what they know, so nothing gets entered twice.

What stays the same

  • Your email, drive, CRM, and phone accounts stay yours.
  • Drafts are for your approval. Nothing sends on your behalf unless you turn that on.
  • In most cases it doesn't replace your software. It connects what you already use.
