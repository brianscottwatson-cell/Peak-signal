## 1. Concept Overview
Peak Signal is an AI implementation partnership. We come alongside a business, understand what they are doing and trying to accomplish, gain access to their data, and build a harness of agents that creates leverage inside their operations.

The core offer is not "custom agents." It is a partnership: retainer as the floor, value-share as the upside, tied to measurable growth in the workflows we touch.

## 2. The Model
- **Free AI Assessment** (lead magnet): structured intake → one-page scorecard (leverage points, data readiness, quick wins). Ends with a clear next step.
- **One-click access handoff**: client grants access to named systems (OneDrive, QuickBooks, CRM, etc.). The click is the intent signal; it auto-scopes the estimate.
- **Auto-scoped estimate**: retainer floor + value-share percentage, expiring in 48 hours.
- **Paid pilot (wedge)**: automate ONE painful workflow end-to-end, done-for-you, result in a week. Proof sells the rest of the harness.
- **Ongoing partnership**: retainer covers access + build; percentage on growth above baseline.

**Baseline rule:** measure against where they started, not a guessed number. Instrument before touching anything.

## 3. Pricing
- Retainer floor: ~$2k–$5k/month per client (hands-on level).
- Value share: percentage of revenue/hours/margin from the AI-touched workflows only — never whole-company revenue.
- Floor covers time; everything above is pure upside for both sides.

## 4. Scaling Path (Project → $1M)
**Phase 1 — Project (now):** Run free assessments yourself on 5–10 real clients. Build harnesses. Charge paid pilots. Collect the scars that become the playbook.

**Phase 2 — Productize:** Fixed packages. Turn repeated builds into templates. Launch the intake agent so the free assessment runs without you.

**Phase 3 — Leverage:** Hire one junior to own discovery + delivery against the playbook. You stay on sales and high-value deals. Agents handle repetitive work underneath.

**Phase 4 — The Million:** 10–15 clients at $3k–$5k/month retainers + percentage upside = ~$500k–$1M/year. Agents + junior do most delivery.

## 5. The Factory (meta-layer)
Build the AI factory that builds the machine: a pipeline of narrow agents, each with fixed input, fixed output, and a "done" checklist. A human only reviews handoffs.
- Record yourself doing the work once → that becomes the spec.
- First real test: the second client. If the same agents produce a harness from a new intake + new data without rewriting, you have something.
- The factory's first real test is the second client.

## 6. Agent Architecture (Grok Bot)
- Build the full team on your master account (Chief of Staff + specialists).
- Generate a share link. Client adds the Bot to their own Grok Bot → gets a copy of profile, skills, routines.
- They do NOT get your cloud computer, logins, or memory.
- You keep the master, the data access, and manage the flock: update routines, add tools, fix drift.
- **Hard rule:** strip every API key, internal URL, and client secret before sharing. The link is public.

## 7. Shared Knowledge Base (G-Stack / G-Brain)
All agents and you communicate through a shared knowledge base in Drive (and the brain repo). Empty files are fine — they are the contract. Talk to any agent; the knowledge base keeps the team on the same page.

## 8. Moat & Cannibalization
- Legitimate and expanding: BCG projects up to $200B in new tech-services demand from agentic AI; 75% of enterprises want outside help.
- Platforms (Microsoft, OpenAI, Amazon) are hiring consultants and embedding services — moving into the same space, but slower than 6 months.
- Model layer commoditizes fast. The hard part stays: client's data, workflows, politics, and trust to keep agents running after launch.
- Real defense: compounding client relationships + proprietary playbooks, not the agents themselves. Each client makes the next one cheaper.
